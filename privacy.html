Book Archive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

About Book Archive

Book Archive is a privately operated, locally hosted application for organizing and managing a personal digital book collection.

Information Processed

Book Archive processes ebook files, book metadata, cover artwork, library organization information, and optional Google account authorization information.

Google Drive Access

Google Drive integration is optional. When authorized, Book Archive uses Google Drive access to upload, retrieve, organize, and manage files created or opened through its integration, including selected ebooks, artwork, and library recovery information.

Data Storage

Book Archive stores its library catalog and application configuration locally. When Google Drive integration is enabled, selected library files and recovery information are also stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Data Sharing

Book Archive does not sell personal information or share Google user data with advertisers. Google Drive information is used to provide the application's requested backup, recovery, and file-management features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Library information remains stored until the user removes it. Users can manage local library records and supported Google Drive files through Book Archive. Google Drive files can also be managed directly through Google Drive.

Security

Book Archive is designed for private, local use. Google authorization credentials are maintained locally and are not published on this website.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application operator through the contact information provided in the application's Google OAuth consent configuration.
